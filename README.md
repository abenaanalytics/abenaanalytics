## 🚀 About Me 
I’m a Business Analyst who is passionate about turning business challenges into practical, data-driven solutions. I enjoy uncovering insights from data to identify problems, understand their root causes, and recommend solutions that deliver measurable business value.

My focus is helping organisations achieve their goals by understanding business needs, analysing processes, and translating complex challenges into clear, actionable recommendations.

Outside of work, I enjoy cooking, mentoring young leaders, travelling, and exploring history. One of the most rewarding parts of business analysis for me is the "eureka!" moment when data reveals an insight that leads to better decisions.
